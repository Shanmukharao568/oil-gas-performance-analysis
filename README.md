📖 Project Overview
The Oil & Gas industry generates vast amounts of operational and financial data daily. However, data only becomes valuable when it is transformed into meaningful insights that support better business decisions.

This project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow using Power BI, from data preparation and modeling to dashboard development. The objective was to analyze financial performance and operational efficiency, enabling stakeholders to monitor business performance, identify operational risks, and make data-driven decisions.

Business Objectives
This project answers key business questions, including:

Which regions and countries contribute the most revenue?
How does operating cost compare with revenue and profit?
Which wells contribute the highest production volume?
Which operational issues are affecting production?
How effectively is maintenance being carried out?
Are older assets associated with higher operating costs?
What insights can be gained from pipeline status and operational incidents?

💡 Key Insights
Revenue reached $11.5M, generating $3.7M in profit.
A small number of wells contributed a significant share of total production.
Maintenance coverage stood at 43%, indicating that less than half of the equipment received maintenance during the reporting period.
Older assets generally showed higher operating costs.
The night shift recorded the highest number of operational incidents.
Most pipelines remained operational, while a smaller proportion were inactive or under maintenance.
📌 Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis:

Prioritize preventive maintenance for aging equipment.
Investigate operational activities during the night shift to reduce incident occurrence.
Closely monitor high-producing wells to sustain production performance.
Improve maintenance scheduling to increase maintenance coverage.
Continuously monitor pipeline status to minimize production disruptions.
